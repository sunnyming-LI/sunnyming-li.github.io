About

I studied architecture at Tongji University, graduating with a Bachelor of Architecture in July 2026. I am currently a research assistant at its College of Architecture and Urban Planning.

My interest in computational design grew through making. Working on furniture that could transform between a chair, stool, and bench prompted me to look more closely at how geometry, structural behavior, and fabrication affect one another. Through subsequent projects in deployable structures and robotic assembly, I became increasingly interested in the workflows that bring a design into physical form.

My current research focuses on computational path generation for willow weaving, considering structural information alongside material and fabrication constraints. More broadly, I’m interested in how feedback between design, analysis, and making can help us revise both a design and the process used to produce it.
