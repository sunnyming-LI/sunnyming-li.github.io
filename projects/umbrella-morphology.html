Umbrella Morphology

Vector-based graphic statics in deployable architectural design

This project investigates how an umbrella-like structure can accommodate folding motion, structural behavior, and practical assembly requirements. Folding simulation and structural analysis informed the joints, panel clearances, and cable assistance of a working full-scale prototype. Future work considers motorized deployment and closer feedback between crease design and structural analysis.

Workflow

From Form-Finding to Physical Construction

  1. Design

    Yoshimura and square-twist patterns generate four families. Parametric transformations and constrained Kangaroo simulations explore their motion.

  2. Analysis

    Form and force diagrams evaluate structural behavior across folding states; fold angles, panel positions, speed, and self-intersection are checked.

  3. Construction

    Panel, frame, hinge, support, and actuation details are integrated into a physical umbrella assembly.

Detail Build

Structural Insights Guide Final Assembly

Optimization

Tensioned cables assist folding and reduce demands on critical members. Panel clearance depends on thickness t, hinge offset r, and included angle θ.

θcrit=2arctan(t2r)

θ > θcrit: no self-intersection.
θ ≤ θcrit: panels intersect.

Additional optimization views (3)

Details

Replaceable rigid canopy panels sit within aluminum frames and hinges. An aluminum tube and ABS core connect the canopy to the column; cables and levers drive folding, above an octagonal base with lockable wheels.
