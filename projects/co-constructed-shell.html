Co-Constructed Shell

Multi-robot collaborative assembly of reciprocal shells

This project connects parametric reciprocal-shell geometry with assembly sequences and a three-robot construction demonstration. My contribution covers geometric modeling, manual sequence studies, demonstration collection, and component details.

Workflow

From Computational Generation to Automated Robotic Construction

  1. Design

    Unit construction and form variations feed surface form-finding and remeshing to produce reciprocal-shell geometries.

  2. Learning

    Manual construction is decomposed into atomic actions, programmable sequences, and repeated loops; demonstrations provide data for task learning.

  3. Construction

    Vehicle and camera hardware, printed parts, and end-effectors are combined with SLAM, point clouds, QR positioning, visual guidance, UR RTDE control, and motion planning.

Parametric Design

Parametric Strategies for Generating Reciprocal Shell Geometries

Reciprocal Frame Research

Hexagonal, quadrilateral, and triangular units are derived through rotation by angle a, translation by offset x, or both. The center-to-center method scales the polygon, connects the original and scaled edge midpoints to intersection points, then offsets those intersections to create eccentric connections.

Task Decomposing

The action study includes pushing the lower plank into the upper plank’s slot, pushing the upper plank into the lower plank’s slot, and bypassing from below. Insertion, lifting, and bypass describe the elementary movements.
